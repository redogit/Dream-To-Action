# Decision Field Profile — Dream to Action

Canonical method: `redogit/redogit/DECISION_FIELD_CORE_2026-09-17.md`.

This profile does not change the prototype's person-directed boundary.

```text
X = user-declared possible next actions / current conditions
O = the person's chosen goal and stop conditions
D = consequential barriers, support, consent, access and readiness distinctions
R = dependency, ownership, support-status, observation and chronology relations
F = record, validate, inspect feasibility, choose, act externally by the person, observe, revise, export
E = self-report provenance plus explicit unverified/illustrative labels
G = the person's declared next bounded result or a clearer unresolved state
U = unconfirmed support, unknown feasibility, unresolved observations, external conditions
```

## Allowed-function boundary

The app itself may:

```text
record
validate fields
surface contradictions/dependencies
preserve snapshots
link observations to plans
export
```

It does not itself:

```text
supply housing/employment/funding/care/legal rights
obtain another person's agreement
schedule reminders
verify real-world feasibility or safety
choose the person's goal
```

Therefore:

```text
PERSON_CHOOSES_GOAL
TOOL_TRANSFORMS_REPRESENTATION
OBSERVATION_UPDATES_FIELD
PLANNER_COMPLETION != REAL_WORLD_OUTCOME
```
